Modeling the Lafayette commute

I combined Census commuting records with a routable street network to model how trips move between eight Lafayette zones. Adding travel time reduced the average in-sample error from 12.98 to 8.38 job connections per zone pair.

M.S. thesis · Traffic & safety

Predicting crash clearance time in Louisiana

I tested clearance-time models on 16,815 Louisiana crash records. Holding out a geographic area lowered the spatial random forest’s macro F1 score from 0.541 to 0.467. The result matters when estimating performance on another corridor.

Coordinating game-day traffic

For a university football scenario, I used SET-IT to define how traffic operators, transit dispatchers, and parking systems would share information before, during, and after a game. The report documents the proposed operating scenarios and system architecture.
