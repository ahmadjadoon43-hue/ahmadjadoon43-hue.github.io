M.S. thesis · Traffic & safety

Louisiana incident clearance time modeling

Traditional and spatial classification of clearance time using 16,815 final LADOTD crash records from 2017–2021.

What I worked on

  • Compared random and geographic validation and interpreted contributing variables with SHAP.
  • Spatial random forest macro F1 was 0.541 under random validation and 0.467 under geographic validation.

Project context

Retrospective research; some predictors are recorded after the event. Crash-level records are not distributed.
