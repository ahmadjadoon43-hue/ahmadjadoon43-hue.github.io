Independent project · Planning & GIS

Lafayette four-step travel demand model

I combined Census commuting records with a routable street network to model how trips move between eight Lafayette zones. Adding travel time reduced the average in-sample error from 12.98 to 8.38 job connections per zone pair.

Read technical report ↗

How much does travel time explain about where people work?

Compare the employment-only baseline with the travel-time model, then inspect how the resulting trips were assigned to the road network.

What I worked on

  • Integrated ACS, LODES, QGIS, and Python; implemented gravity distribution, aggregate mode allocation, and all-or-nothing assignment.
  • Reduced in-sample MAE from 12.98 to 8.38 relationships per O-D cell and checked demand conservation and scenario sensitivity.

Project context

Educational pilot; no independent traffic-count validation. External, nonwork, and routed intrazonal demand are excluded.
