Johnston Street crash impact and Tableau analysis

I used 43,200 one-minute probe records to compare speeds on Johnston Street during the January 6 closure with the same times on January 5 and 7. The two directions tell different stories.

Read the study (PDF)

What I worked on

  • Organized directional segments and aggregated speeds into 15-minute intervals; inspected repeated and estimated probe speeds.
  • Built a Tableau Story of five dashboards with filter, highlight, and URL actions; identified northbound disruption until about 10:30 AM.

Project context

INRIX data were instructor-provided. The raw data and packaged Tableau workbook are not redistributed; estimated closure-period speeds limit recovery inference.
